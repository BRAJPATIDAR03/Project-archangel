⚡ Embedded Learning Tricks (Used Throughout)
🧠

Feynman Technique

After every topic, explain it in plain English as if teaching a 10-year-old. Instant gap detector.

🃏

Spaced Repetition (Anki)

10 min/day reviewing flashcards. Defeats the forgetting curve. Never lose what you've learned.

🔨

Build To Learn

Every concept has a mini-project. No tutorial hell. Learn by breaking things and fixing them.

🔁

Interleaving

Mix topics daily (code + theory + project). Proven to create 25% better retention than block study.

⏱️

Pomodoro 25/5

25 min deep work, 5 min break. Repeat 4×, then take a 30 min break. Maximizes focus.

🎯

Outcome-First Learning

Always know WHAT job you're building a skill for. Context makes learning 3× faster.
